---
title: The Cat Sleeps...Still
date: 2026-07-30
tags: music, cats, ambient, tapes
---

**I once went by the handle sleepingbasementcat**. I took the name after the cat we had, once found sleeping in the basement of our house, looking peaceful and adorable. I used the handle for some of the music I was making at the time. I was messing around with tapes (yes, cassette tapes), sampling, mangling, chopping and screwing up sounds and passing them on to a friend who would layer more stuff on top, or make something for the other side of the tape.

Once I even got a track on a [massive compilation for RRRecords](https://www.discogs.com/release/94233-Various-RRR-500-Various-500-Lock-Grooves-By-500-Artists). I never really did much after that because of life events. Somehow one of those co-produced tapes was chronicled on a [site featuring music from Ontario in the 90s](http://livingarchive.doncampau.com/cassettes_international/canada) (about halfway down). 

Fast forward more years than I care to count, and I've been returning to the idea of making some kind of music/noise. I've been playing around with [VCV rack](https://vcvrack.com/) and finally decided to actually record some of the sounds coming out of it and post them somewhere. Of course, choosing a name for the project was a no brainer, so the basement cat sleeps again...still.
